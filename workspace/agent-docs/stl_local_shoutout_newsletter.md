## Main Feature: Pappy's Smokehouse

**Category:** BBQ Restaurant  
**Address:** 3106 Olive St, St. Louis, MO 63103 (Midtown)  
**Website:** [pappyssmokehouse.com](https://pappyssmokehouse.com)

### The Story
Pappy's has been a St. Louis institution since 2008, serving up Memphis-style dry-rubbed ribs that have earned national recognition. Featured on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives, Man v. Food, and just about every "best BBQ" list in America. But locals know it's more than the fame—it's the consistency, the smoke, and the line out the door that proves it's the real deal.

### What Makes It Special
- **The ribs** — Dry-rubbed with their secret spice blend, smoked over apple and cherry wood for 4+ hours
- **The sides** — Don't skip the sweet potato fries or fried corn on the cob
- **The sauce** — Six house-made sauces on every table, from sweet to spicy

### Why We Love It
There's something magical about waiting in line at Pappy's. You strike up conversations with strangers who become friends, all bonded by the promise of fall-off-the-bone ribs. It's St. Louis community at its finest—plus, the food absolutely delivers on the hype.

**Pro tip:** Get there early (they open at 11am). When they sell out, they close. On busy weekends, that can be mid-afternoon.

## This Weekend: City Foundry STL Food Hall

**Type:** Food Hall & Entertainment  
**Address:** 3730 Foundry Way, St. Louis, MO 63110 (Midtown)

### Why It's Perfect for This Weekend
The City Foundry is your one-stop summer destination. With 10+ local food vendors under one roof (and plenty of patio seating), you can satisfy every craving in your group. Plus, the outdoor plaza often has live music and events on weekends.

### Best Time to Go
Saturday or Sunday afternoon, 2-6pm. Beat the lunch rush, grab a drink, and take your time exploring.

### Perfect For
Groups who can't agree on what to eat, date nights, or when you want variety without driving all over town.

## 3 Hidden Gem BBQ & Smoke Spots

Because one BBQ feature isn't enough for St. Louis:

### 1. Bogart's Smokehouse
- **What:** St. Louis-style BBQ with a twist
- **Where:** 1627 S 9th St, Soulard
- **Why Go:** The "Pitmaster Sandwich"—pulled pork, brisket, AND smoked sausage on one bun. Burnt end appetizer is legendary.

### 2. Beast Craft BBQ Co.
- **What:** Texas-style craft BBQ
- **Where:** 20 S Belt W, Belleville, IL (worth the short drive!)
- **Why Go:** Brisket that rivals Austin. House-made sausages change weekly. Craft beer selection is top-notch.

### 3. Salt + Smoke
- **What:** Upscale BBQ with creative sides
- **Where:** Multiple locations (Delmar Loop, CWE, Ballpark Village)
- **Why Go:** The "White Cheddar Cracker Mac" is famous for a reason. Great cocktails and a more polished atmosphere.

## Quick Picks

🍺 **4 Hands Brewing** (1220 S 8th St) — Local craft brewery with a great patio. Try the City Wide Pilsner, their flagship beer that supports local charities.

🍕 **Pi Pizzeria** (multiple locations) — Deep dish AND thin crust options. President Obama's favorite when he visits STL. The cornmeal crust is the secret weapon.

🌮 **Mission Taco Joint** (Delmar Loop, Soulard, CWE) — California-style street tacos with a St. Louis twist. Happy hour margaritas are dangerous (in the best way).

☕ **Rise Coffee House** (4180 Manchester Ave, The Grove) — Third-wave coffee in a cozy, community-focused space. Great for remote work or catching up with friends.
